Scouting report

A 1.94 m guard who can play on and off the ball. Attacks the basket with an explosive first step and finishes well through contact, comfortable as a secondary ball handler in pick-and-roll. Defends the opponent's best perimeter player and communicates well in team defence.

Strengths

  • Strong driver; draws fouls and creates for teammates off penetration
  • Explosive first step; attacks the rim and finishes through traffic
  • Point-of-attack defence; length to contest shots
  • Vocal leader; team captain and loudest communicator on the court
  • Low turnover rate (1.4 per game) as a secondary handler
  • Relentless drive to improve; studies film and absorbs every piece of coaching

Working on

  • Pull-up mid-range jumper off the dribble
  • Perimeter shooting consistency; developing catch-and-shoot accuracy
  • Adding strength: +4 kg target over the summer

Best fit

A rim-attacking two guard with great court vision and solid defence on the perimeter.
